## What is an EduKora sheet?

A sheet is **one notion to know by heart**: its definition in a few lines, sometimes an example, and **the years it came up in the exam**. It is written by EduKora, from the solutions of past papers.

It is shorter than a lesson: it is what you reread the day before the exam.

## Where do I find them?

In "Revise", **Sheets** tab, or from [My notes](/carnet). Open the [EduKora sheets](/fiches-edukora), choose your subject, and search for a notion if needed.

Sheets are made **for your exam**: if you haven't said which one you are preparing, choose it first.

## How are they different from my notes?

- **My notes** are what **you** write.
- **EduKora sheets** are what **EduKora** wrote for you.

They meet: "Keep in my notes" copies a sheet into the folder of its subject. It then joins your review: you are asked the notion and recall the definition from memory. See [My notes](/aide/mes-notes).

## What does "Came up in 2018, 2019" mean?

That the notion was asked in the official exam in those years. A notion that came up several times deserves to be learned first: sheets are sorted in that order. Mock exams are shown separately.

## Free or paid?

A few sheets per subject can be read **without a subscription**. For the others, you see the notion and the years it came up; the definition needs the subscription. See [What does the subscription include?](/aide/contenu-abonnement).

## Why does my subject have no sheets?

They arrive **subject by subject**: each sheet is reviewed by a teacher before it is published.
