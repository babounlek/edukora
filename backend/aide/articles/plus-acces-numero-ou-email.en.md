Your account and your subscription are tied to the **number or address** you log in with. If you can no longer reach it (lost phone, replaced SIM card, closed mailbox), you can't receive a code.

## What to do

1. Try your **other** login method if you had one (e-mail or Google).
2. Otherwise, write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}}. Tell us:
   - the old number or the old address,
   - your exam,
   - whether you have already paid for a subscription.
3. The team checks that the account really is yours before doing anything.

Your subscription is **not lost**: it stays on your account.

See also [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support).
