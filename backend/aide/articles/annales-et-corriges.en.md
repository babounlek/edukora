## Where to find them

In the papers list, filter by **subject** and by **year**. By default, recently added papers come first.

## Free or paid?

Some corrections are open to everyone. The others need a subscription. A label on each paper shows what you can open: see [Free or paid?](/aide/gratuit-ou-payant).

## The topics that come back

Rather than reading every year, the **frequent topics** page ranks the notions that real past papers ask the most. Ideal to know where to start.

## A mistake in a correction?

Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with the subject, the year and the question number. A teacher reviews the corrections that are reported.
