Instead of asking you what to revise, {{site}} offers you **one session a day**, made for your exam.

## What a session contains

A topic of the day, with the steps to work on it: read the method, practise, check. The steps appear on the home page.

## How to do it

1. Open the home page.
2. Press "Start" on the session card.
3. Follow the steps. When you are done, the session is marked as done and your progress moves forward.

## Does it always offer the same topic?

No: topics come back according to **what you have already succeeded at** and what comes back most in real past papers. A topic you miss reappears later.
