## Downloading

On a paper or a lesson, use the download button. The PDF file is prepared and then downloaded: the first time, it can take a few seconds.

## The daily limit

So that everyone can benefit, the number of downloads is **limited each day**. When you reach your limit, a message tells you **at what time** the quota resets: you can then download again.

## Need more?

The subscription opens a wider limit than free access: see [What does the subscription include?](/aide/contenu-abonnement).

## Without downloading

You don't need a PDF to revise: everything can be read online, and what you have read stays available offline. See [Reading offline](/aide/lire-hors-ligne).
