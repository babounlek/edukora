## What it opens

- **Full corrections** of past papers, unlimited.
- **Practice exercises** solved step by step, after each lesson.
- The **quiz** that finds your weak spots and brings them back, and your **daily session**.
- The {{site}} **mock papers**.
- The ranking of the **topics that come up most** in real past papers.

Everything is included, whatever number of months you choose.

## How long?

You choose the number of months when you pay: **from 1 month up to the end of your exam**. One month is 30 days of access. The total to pay and the **end date of your access** are shown before you pay, on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page as well as on the subscription page.

The maximum number of months stops at the end of your exam: you never pay for months after it. The longest option is called "Until the exam".

## One exam only

The subscription is tied to the exam you chose when paying. Check it first: see [Choosing or changing my exam](/aide/choisir-son-examen).

## Renewal

There is none: nothing is ever charged automatically. When your access ends, you decide whether to extend it: the new months are added after the current ones.

## The price

The price is **per month and per child**, the same for every exam. It is on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page, which also shows current offers with their end date.
