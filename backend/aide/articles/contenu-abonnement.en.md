There is **only one plan**: the **monthly** subscription. You choose the number of months and pay in one go.

## What it opens

- **Full corrections** of past papers, unlimited.
- **Practice exercises** solved step by step, after each lesson.
- The **quiz** that finds your weak spots and brings them back, and your **daily session**.
- The {{site}} **mock papers**.
- All the **[EduKora sheets](/aide/fiches-edukora)** for your exam, to keep in your notes or print (without a subscription, only a few sheets per subject can be read).
- The ranking of the **topics that come up most** in real past papers.

Everything is included, whatever number of months you choose.

## How long?

You choose the number of months when you pay: **from 1 month up to the end of your exam**. One month is 30 days of access. The total to pay and the **end date of your access** are shown before you pay, on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page as well as on the payment screen.

The maximum number of months stops at the end of your exam: you never pay for months after it. The longest option is called "Until the exam": it is not a different plan, it is the same monthly subscription, with all the months left before the end of your exam.

If you bought the former "Until the exam" subscription before 11 November 2026, it remains valid until its planned end date.

## One exam only

The subscription is tied to the exam you chose when paying. Check it first: see [Choosing or changing my exam](/aide/choisir-son-examen).

## Renewal

There is none: nothing is ever charged automatically. When your access ends, you decide whether to extend it: the new months are added after the current ones.

## The price

The price is **per month and per child**, the same for every exam. Every child in the same family pays the same price. It is on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page, which also shows current offers with their end date.
