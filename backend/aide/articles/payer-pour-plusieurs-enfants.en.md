Each child has their **own profile**, with their progress and their subscription.

## Adding a child

From [My access](/mes-acces), choose "Add a child". You enter their exam, then pay for their subscription.

## Price

Every child pays **the same price**: there is no discount for the second child or the following ones. The exact amount is shown on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page: it changes with offers, so that page is always the reference.

## Paying for a child

In [My access](/mes-acces), each child is marked "Subscribed" or "To pay". The child's pay button takes you to payment: see [How to pay](/aide/comment-payer).

## Protecting the profiles

So that children can't open each other's profiles, see [Profiles and parent code](/aide/profils-et-code-parent).
