## Reading a lesson

Lessons are **free**. Find them by subject in the lessons list, or with search: see [Searching {{site}}](/aide/recherche).

## Practice exercises

After each lesson come **progressive** exercises with detailed solutions. They need a subscription: see [What does the subscription include?](/aide/contenu-abonnement).

## Keeping a passage or writing a note

While reading, you can **keep a section** for later or write a **note**. Everything is in your [Notebook](/carnet).

## A lesson doesn't match my question?

Tell us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with the lesson title. It helps us fix the links between quizzes, lessons and exercises.
