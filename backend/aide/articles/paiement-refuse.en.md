A refused payment is not a dead end, and **a failed payment is not charged**.

## The request on your phone failed

The operator does not always tell us why. Almost every failure comes from one of these causes:

- **Insufficient balance**: your Mobile Money account must cover the total shown on the payment screen.
- **Wrong PIN**: start the payment again and enter it once more, without rushing.
- **Expired request**: it is only valid for a few minutes. Start the payment again and approve it right away.
- **Request never shown**: dial **\*126#** on MTN or **#150\*50#** on Orange to find it and approve it.
- **Number refused**: only **Orange** and **MTN** numbers with an active Mobile Money account are accepted.

Then press "Try again": you keep your exam and your number of months.

**You approved, but the screen says the confirmation is taking long?** Do not pay a second time. Your access is activated as soon as the operator confirms, even if you closed the page. If the screen tells you a request is already waiting on your number, approve that one.

## Your declared transfer was refused

When manual transfer is offered and you declared one, the **reason** for the refusal is shown in [My payments](/mes-paiements), under the payment concerned.

- **Transaction not found**: the declared ID matches no transfer. Re-read your operator's SMS and copy the ID exactly.
- **Wrong amount**: the transferred amount is not the total shown on the payment screen (the monthly price times the number of months chosen).
- **Wrong operator**: the declared operator (Orange or MTN) is not the one used for the transfer.
- **Transaction already used**: this transaction was already used for another access.
- **Payment not received**: the payment did not reach our account. Check that you paid the number or merchant code shown.

Fix the cause and **declare your transaction again** from the payment page.
