## Free

- All the **lessons**, to read in full.
- Some **past-paper corrections** are open to everyone: they are marked in the lists.
- **Trial quizzes**, even without creating an account.

## With the subscription

- **Full corrections**, unlimited.
- **Practice exercises** with step-by-step solutions, after each lesson.
- The **quiz that targets your revision** and your full **daily session**.
- The **mock papers**.

Details, current offers and the price are on the [Pricing](/tarifs) page. The price is **per month** (you choose the number of months), with no automatic renewal: see [How to pay](/aide/comment-payer).

## Something is greyed out?

A padlock or a greyed button means this content needs the subscription. Read [Why is my session locked?](/aide/seance-verrouillee).
