Payment is by **Mobile Money** (Orange Money or MTN MoMo), **in one go** for the number of months you chose. There is no bank card, no automatic debit and no renewal: nothing is charged again unless you do it yourself.

## Before you start

1. [Log in](/connexion).
2. Open the [Pricing](/tarifs) page and choose your exam: the price and the end date of your access are shown.
3. Click "Subscribe".

## Method 1: automatic payment

Enter your Mobile Money number. A **payment request arrives on your phone**: confirm it with your secret code. Your access is activated as soon as the operator confirms.

## Method 2: manual payment

If automatic payment doesn't work, or isn't offered:

1. Transfer the **exact amount** to the number shown on the payment page. The beneficiary's name is shown too: check it before sending.
2. Keep your operator's confirmation SMS.
3. Come back to the page and **declare your transaction** (operator, number, transaction ID).
4. Our team checks your transfer, usually within {{delai_verification}}, then activates your access. You receive a confirmation SMS.

Follow the progress in [My payments](/mes-paiements): see [My payment is being verified](/aide/paiement-en-verification).
