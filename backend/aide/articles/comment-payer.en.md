Payment is by **Mobile Money** (Orange Money or MTN MoMo), **in one go** for the number of months you chose. There is no bank card, no automatic debit and no renewal: nothing is charged again unless you do it yourself.

## Before you pay

1. Open the [Pricing](/tarifs) page.
2. Choose your exam, then the number of months: from 1 month up to your exam. The total to pay and the end date of your access are shown.
3. Press the "Continue" button: it repeats the number of months and the total.
4. If you are not logged in yet, log in or create your account, with your phone number, your e-mail address or Google: see [Creating my account and logging in](/aide/creer-un-compte).
5. You arrive on the payment screen.

## Paying: the request arrives on your phone

1. On the payment screen, enter the **Orange or MTN number that will pay** (9 digits, starting with 6), then press "Pay".
2. A **payment request arrives on that phone**, showing the amount. It is sent by **CamPay**, our Mobile Money payment provider, and shows **the name TAKWID GROUP**, the company that runs CamPay: that is normal. Check the amount, then approve with your **Mobile Money PIN**. You type that PIN only on your phone: see [Is my payment safe? Who are CamPay and TAKWID GROUP?](/aide/paiement-securise).
3. Your access opens as soon as the operator confirms, usually within seconds. You receive a confirmation SMS, and the payment appears in [My payments](/mes-paiements).

**Nothing showing on your phone?** Dial **\*126#** on MTN or **#150\*50#** on Orange: you will find the pending request there and can approve it. The code to dial is also shown on the payment screen.

**Approved a bit late?** Do not pay a second time. The screen keeps checking for about ten minutes, and your access is activated anyway as soon as the operator confirms, even if you closed the page.

## Paying by making the transfer yourself, when it is offered

This method is not always available: the payment screen tells you whether it is.

1. On the payment screen, choose Orange Money or MTN MoMo.
2. Send the **exact amount** to the number shown, or pay it to the **merchant code** if a code is shown instead. The beneficiary's name is shown too: check that your operator displays this name before entering your PIN.
3. Keep your operator's confirmation SMS.
4. Come back to the payment screen, press "I've made the payment", then **declare your payment**: the number that paid and the **transaction number** written in your operator's SMS.
5. Our team checks your transfer, usually within {{delai_verification}}, then your access is activated. You receive a confirmation SMS.

Follow the progress in [My payments](/mes-paiements): see [My payment is being verified](/aide/paiement-en-verification).

## Before you pay, check

The exam and the number of months: a confirmed payment is not refundable, except in case of a proven technical malfunction of the platform. See [Can I get a refund?](/aide/remboursement).
