## The weekly summary

If you have confirmed your e-mail address, you receive a summary **every Sunday evening**: how many days your child revised and what they consolidated.

This summary contains only **facts**, with no judgement and no comparison with other students. If your child didn't revise, the message simply says so and suggests the shortest way to start again. A family with several children receives **a single message** for all profiles.

You can stop these messages in one click from the link at the bottom of the e-mail.

## Each child's access

The [My access](/mes-acces) page shows, for each child, whether they are subscribed or still to subscribe, and until when.

## Seeing what your child sees

You don't see more than your child sees: the summary is computed from their own progress.
