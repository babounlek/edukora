## What works offline

Papers and lessons you have **already read** on this device stay available without a network. Use the "Read offline" button on a paper or lesson to keep it.

## Installing {{site}} on your phone

From your browser menu, choose "Add to home screen" or "Install". {{site}} then opens like an app, faster.

## "Unable to keep it offline"

This message means your browser could not save the page on the device. Try, in order:

1. Check you have enough **space** on your phone.
2. **Reload** the page with a connection, then try again.
3. Use your phone's usual browser (Chrome or equivalent) rather than one built into another app.
4. Still nothing? Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}}, saying which phone and browser you use.

## What needs a connection

Quizzes, the daily session, login and payment all need a network.
