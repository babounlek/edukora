Your subscription is tied to **your account**. You can therefore log in from a phone, a computer or a tablet: you just receive a code at each new login.

## What stays on the device

The papers and lessons you have read stay available **offline** on that device, but not on the others. See [Reading offline](/aide/lire-hors-ligne).

## On a device that isn't yours

In an internet cafe or at a friend's, remember to **log out** from [My account](/compte) when you leave.

## Sharing an account

Your subscription is personal. For a brother or sister who is also preparing an exam, the easiest way is to create their **own profile**: see [Several children, several profiles](/aide/profils-et-code-parent).
