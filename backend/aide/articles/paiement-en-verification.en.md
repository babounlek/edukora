"Being verified" means **your declaration arrived** and our team is checking your transfer.

## What you need to do

**Nothing.** Don't make the transfer again: you would pay twice. Your access is activated automatically once validated, and you receive an SMS.

## How long?

Usually within **{{delai_verification}}**. Follow the progress on the [My payments](/mes-paiements) page: a three-step bar (declared, being verified, access activated).

## After the deadline

Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with:

- your **support code** (see [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support)),
- the **transaction ID** from your operator's SMS,
- the **amount** and **time** of the transfer.

With these three details, we find your payment right away.
