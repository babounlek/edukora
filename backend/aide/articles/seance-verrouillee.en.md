A **padlock** or a greyed button means this content needs the subscription. It is not a bug.

## What stays free

- All the **lessons**.
- Some **past-paper corrections**.
- **Trial quizzes**.

See the details in [Free or paid?](/aide/gratuit-ou-payant).

## You paid but it is still locked

1. Open [My payments](/mes-paiements): if the payment is "being verified", access is not active yet. See [My payment is being verified](/aide/paiement-en-verification).
2. If the payment is validated, check in [My access](/mes-acces) that the **right child** and the **right exam** are selected: the subscription is tied to a profile and an exam.
3. Reload the page or log in again.
4. Still locked? Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with your support code (see [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support)).
