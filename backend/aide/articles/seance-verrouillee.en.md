A **padlock** or a greyed button means this content needs the subscription. It is not a bug.

## What stays free

- All the **lessons**.
- Some **past-paper corrections**.
- **Trial quizzes**.
- A few **EduKora sheets** per subject.

See the details in [Free or paid?](/aide/gratuit-ou-payant).

## A sheet is locked

On a locked EduKora sheet, you see the **notion** and the **years it came up**. The definition and the rest of the sheet (example, trap, exercise) need the subscription. While it is locked, you can neither keep it in your notes nor print it.

The sheets open without a subscription are there for you to try: there are a few in each subject. See [EduKora sheets](/aide/fiches-edukora).

## You paid but it is still locked

1. Open [My payments](/mes-paiements): if the payment is "being verified", access is not active yet. See [My payment is being verified](/aide/paiement-en-verification).
2. If the payment is validated, check in [My access](/mes-acces) that the **right child** and the **right exam** are selected: the subscription is tied to a profile and an exam. So are the sheets: they open for the exam you paid for.
3. Reload the page or log in again.
4. Still locked? Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with your support code (see [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support)).
