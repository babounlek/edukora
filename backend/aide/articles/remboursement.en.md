## The rule

Payments are **final and non-refundable once the transaction is confirmed**, except in case of a **proven technical malfunction** of the platform that prevents you from accessing the content you subscribed to. This is stated in our [terms of use](/cgu).

## Before paying

You can look at what is free, change exam and read the pricing as much as you like: **nothing is committed before payment**. See [Free or paid?](/aide/gratuit-ou-payant).

## If you paid twice by mistake, or your access does not activate

Report it to support: write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with your support code and your operator's SMS. See [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support).

## If you pay on behalf of a parent

Get the money from them **before** sending it: {{site}} refunds nothing until the payment has been verified and validated.
