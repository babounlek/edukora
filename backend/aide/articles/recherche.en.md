Search finds **lessons**, **papers** and **notions**.

## Tips

- No need for accents: "theoreme de thales" works.
- A small typo is corrected: "pytagore" finds "Pythagore".
- Type the notion rather than a sentence: "limits of sequences", "photosynthesis".
- Use the **filters** (subject, exam) to shorten the list.

## I can't find what I'm looking for

1. Try a shorter word or a synonym.
2. Check that your **exam** is the right one in [My account](/compte): the results depend on it.
3. That content may not exist yet: tell us what you were looking for on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}}, that is how we know what to add.
