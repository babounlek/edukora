If your parents are the ones paying, the easiest way is to send them a clear message.

## The "Show my parents" button

It prepares a **ready-made message** for WhatsApp: what the subscription opens, how to pay, the contact, and a link to the details. You can read it and change it before sending.

## What your parent needs to know

- Payment is made by **Mobile Money** (MTN or Orange), in one go, with no automatic debit.
- The price is **per month**: you choose the number of months, from 1 month up to the exam, and the total is shown before paying.
- There is a page for payment questions: [How to pay](/aide/comment-payer).
- {{site}} is published by **{{editeur}}**. The contact is {{whatsapp}} on WhatsApp or {{email}}.

## If you pay with your own number instead of a parent

Get the amount handed to you **before** sending the money: see [Refunds](/aide/remboursement).
