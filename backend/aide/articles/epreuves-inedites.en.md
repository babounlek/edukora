An **original mock paper** is a paper written by {{site}}, in your exam's format and **never seen elsewhere**: perfect to practise without knowing the paper in advance.

## How it works

1. You pick a paper and start it: a **timer** begins.
2. You answer as in the exam.
3. You get a **report**: what you have mastered, what you haven't, and the skills to work on.

## Leaving and coming back

You can leave the paper and come back: your answers and your timer are kept.

## And the mini papers?

A **mini mock paper** is a short version: **two exercises**, a **short timer** and the same marking as the full paper. Handy when you are short of time. One mini paper is featured every week on the subscriber home page.

## Who can open them?

Original mock papers are part of the subscription: see [What does the subscription include?](/aide/contenu-abonnement). Mini papers are reserved for subscribers: an active subscription to your exam is enough.

## Is a low mark a problem?

No. The report is there to **spot** what is left to work on. The topics you missed are offered again in your daily session.
