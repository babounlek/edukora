When you pay with the request that arrives on your phone, the payment goes through **CamPay**, our Mobile Money payment provider. Here is who they are, and what protects your money.

## Who is CamPay?

CamPay is a Cameroonian online payment service, connected to **MTN Mobile Money** and **Orange Money**. It is run by the company Takwid Group and has been operating in Cameroon since 2021.

{{site}} uses CamPay because it sends the payment request straight to your phone: your access opens as soon as your operator confirms, without waiting for someone to check your payment.

## What you see on your phone

After you press "Pay", a request arrives on the phone of the number you entered. CamPay sends it on behalf of {{site}}: depending on your operator, **CamPay's name may appear** instead of ours. That is normal.

Before approving, check that the **amount** in the request is the total shown on the payment screen. If it is different, do not approve it and write to us.

## What protects your money

- **Your PIN stays on your phone.** You type it in your operator's menu, never on the site: neither {{site}} nor CamPay sees it.
- **Nothing is charged unless you approve.** A request you do not approve expires after a few minutes, at no cost to you. A payment that fails is not charged.
- **You pay once.** No bank card, no automatic debit, no renewal: nothing is charged again unless you do it yourself.
- **You keep a proof.** Your operator sends you an SMS after the payment, we send you a confirmation SMS, and the payment shows in [My payments](/mes-paiements).

## Beware of scams

**No one from {{site}} will ever ask for your Mobile Money PIN**, not by phone, not by SMS, not on WhatsApp. If someone asks for it claiming to be {{site}}, it is a scam: do not give it, and let us know.

A subscription is paid only from the site's payment screen. Never transfer money to a number someone gave you by phone or on WhatsApp.

## What if something goes wrong?

- **You approved but your access does not open**: do not pay a second time. See [My payment was refused or failed](/aide/paiement-refuse).
- **You were charged but did not get access**: write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} with your operator's SMS, which is your proof of payment. A proven technical malfunction of the platform entitles you to a refund: see [Can I get a refund?](/aide/remboursement).
