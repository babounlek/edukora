The exam you are preparing (BEPC, Probatoire, BAC, GCE O Level or A Level) and your series decide everything you see: the papers, the lessons, the quizzes and your daily session.

## Choosing it

At the start, {{site}} asks what you are preparing. Answer with your real exam: it sets everything else.

## Changing it

Open [My account](/compte): the exam you are preparing is shown there, and you can change it. You are free to change as often as you like **before paying**.

## Be careful

A subscription is tied to **one specific exam**, until the day of that exam. Before paying, make sure it is the right one: see [What does the subscription include?](/aide/contenu-abonnement).
