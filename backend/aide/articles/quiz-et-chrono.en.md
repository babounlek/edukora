## What is a quiz for?

To spot what you master and what you are missing. The topics you missed are **offered again later**, at the right time.

## Points

A finished quiz earns you points (XP) and moves your streak forward. Trial quizzes, taken without an account, don't count.

## The timer

The timer is **optional**: you can turn it on to practise in exam conditions. The elapsed time is kept if you leave and resume.

## Leaving and resuming

If you leave a quiz before the end, "Resume" takes you back **to where you stopped**, with your answers.

## Reviewing an old quiz

Your past quizzes are in your [history](/historique): you can read them back as you did them.
