## The principle

Becoming a reseller is **free**: no stock to buy, no sign-up fee. Your customers pay the site's **public price**; your code only attributes the sale to you.

## Who can apply?

Sales agents, tutors, schools and centres. The team reviews each application before accepting it.

## How am I paid?

Every month by **Mobile Money** (Orange Money or MTN MoMo), to the number you have verified. The detailed terms are in the reseller charter, which you should read before sending your application.

## What if my application is not accepted?

You receive an SMS with the reason, and you can apply again later.
