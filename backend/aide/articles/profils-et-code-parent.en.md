## One profile per child

In one account, each child has **their own profile**: their progress, their exam and their subscription are separate.

## Why a parent code?

Without a code, each child can see the others' subscriptions and open their profile. A **parent code** (4 digits) keeps that to you: actions reserved for the parent (other children, profile security, adding a child) then ask for this code.

## Setting it up

In [My access](/mes-acces), open the "Profile security" section and follow the steps.

## Protecting a child's profile

A profile can also be protected by its **own 4-digit code**: it must be entered to open it.
