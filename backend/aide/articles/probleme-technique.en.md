## Quick fixes that solve most problems

1. **Reload** the page.
2. Check your **network**: a weak signal gives half-loaded pages.
3. **Close and reopen** the tab, or the app if you installed it.
4. **Update your browser** (Chrome or equivalent).
5. Try **logging in again**: [login](/connexion).

## Maths or physics formulas don't show

Reload the page with a connection. If raw symbols (like `$...$`) stay visible, report it: give the title of the paper or lesson.

## Slow site

{{site}} is designed for limited connections. If a page stays slow, read it once with a connection: it will then be available offline. See [Reading offline](/aide/lire-hors-ligne).

## Still stuck?

Write to us on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}} saying:

- what you were trying to do,
- what you saw (error message, blank page),
- your phone and browser.

See [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support).
