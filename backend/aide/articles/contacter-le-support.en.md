## Reaching us

- **WhatsApp**: {{whatsapp}} (fastest).
- **E-mail**: {{email}}.

## The support code

When you are logged in, your **support code** (it starts with "EK-") appears at the bottom of the help centre, with a button to write to support. It lets the team find your account **without asking for your number or e-mail again**. It is not a password: it gives no access.

## To get a quick answer, include

- your **support code** (or the number/address of your account if you can't log in),
- the **article** you just read, or the page where you are stuck,
- what you tried and what you see,
- for a payment: the **transaction ID**, the **amount**, the **operator** and the **time**.

## Don't

Never send us your **login code**, a password or your Mobile Money secret code. We will never ask for them.
