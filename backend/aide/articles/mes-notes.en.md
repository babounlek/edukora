## What are notes for?

To keep **what you want to remember**: a rule, a trap, a definition, a quiz mistake. Everything is in [My notes](/carnet), sorted into **one folder per subject**.

## How do I write a note?

There are four ways, all with an account:

- **In a lesson or a solution**: select a passage, then tap "Note this". The passage is kept with your note. The "Note" button, at the bottom of the screen, opens a note without a passage.
- **After a quiz question**: tap "Note this mistake" (or "Add to my notes"). You choose to attach the question and the correct answer, or to write freely.
- **On a topic exercise**: the "Note" button and "Note this" work as in a lesson.
- **From My notes or the home page**: "New note", in the subject of your choice.

## Formatting, dictating, adding a photo

Above the text, buttons set **bold**, *italic*, a list, or a formula between `$` (for example `$a^2+b^2=c^2$`). "Preview" shows the result.

The **microphone** writes what you say (you must allow the microphone, and be online). Formulas cannot be dictated.

"Add a photo" attaches an image: a diagram, a page of your exercise book. It is visible **only to you**.

## Finding a note

Open a folder, or use the search: it looks through all your subjects and ignores accents. **Pin** a note to keep it at the top. "Reopen the source" takes you back to the lesson, solution or quiz it comes from.

## Reviewing my notes

My notes tells you **how many notes to review today**. You recall the note from memory, then say whether you remembered it:

- "I remember": it comes back later, further and further apart;
- "Review again": it comes back tomorrow.

Reviewing your notes earns no points: it is for your memory, not for the score.

## Notes that come back at the right time

A note taken in a quiz or on a topic is **attached to that topic**. If you get that topic wrong again, or reopen its exercises, "You have a note on this topic" brings it back in front of you.

## Sharing or printing

"Share" sends a note to a classmate, through WhatsApp for example. The printer, at the top of a folder, prints all the notes of the subject (or saves them as a PDF).

## Deleting a note

Tap "⋯" then "Delete this note". You have a few seconds to **undo**. The photo does not come back.

## I can't see the button to take a note

Notes need an **account**. Without an account, or during a trial quiz, the buttons are not shown. During an unseen paper taken in exam conditions, they are hidden so they don't distract you.
