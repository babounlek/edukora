## What is explained elsewhere

The [Privacy](/confidentialite) page details what data is collected, why, and how long it is kept. The [terms of use](/cgu) explain the platform's rules.

## Reminders and notifications

Revision reminders (e-mail, notification, WhatsApp) are **optional** and can be stopped at any time. Every e-mail contains a link to stop them in one click.

## Seeing, correcting or deleting my data

Write to us at {{email}} from your account's address, or on WhatsApp at {{whatsapp}}. Give your support code (see [Contacting support](/aide/contacter-le-support)) so we can find your account without asking you for more details.
